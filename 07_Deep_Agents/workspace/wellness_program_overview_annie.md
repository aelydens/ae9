## ⚠️ Hip Injury Considerations

### Red Flags - Stop Exercise If You Experience:
- Sharp, shooting pain in hip
- Pain that worsens during movement
- Swelling or significant stiffness
- Pain that persists after exercise

### Green Lights - These Are Normal:
- Mild muscle fatigue after exercise
- Gentle stretching sensations
- Improved range of motion over time
- Temporary muscle soreness 24-48 hours post-workout

### Modification Reminders:
- Use pillows/cushions for support
- Perform seated variations when needed
- Reduce range of motion if uncomfortable
- Focus on form over intensity
