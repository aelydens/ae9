# Annie's 2-Week Mindfulness & Wellness Plan
*Supporting Your Exercise Journey with Mind-Body Wellness*

## Overview
This mindfulness plan complements your exercise program, helping you manage stress, optimize recovery, and maintain a positive relationship with your body during your fitness journey.

---

## Daily Stress Management (5-10 minutes)

### Option 1: Mindful Journaling (5 mins)
- Write about daily thoughts and feelings
- Focus on successes, no matter how small
- Acknowledge any stressors without judgment
- **Best Time:** Evening, after your workout

### Option 2: Grounding Exercise (5 mins)
- Sit or stand comfortably
- Close your eyes, take 5 deep breaths
- Focus on feeling your feet on the ground
- Notice the connection between your body and the surface
- **Best Time:** Before exercise to center yourself

### Option 3: Nature Walk (5-10 mins)
- Short outdoor walk (even around the block)
- Focus on sights, sounds, and smells
- Leave phone behind for true mental break
- **Best Time:** Any time stress levels feel high

---

## Sleep Optimization Routine

### Consistent Schedule
- Same bedtime and wake time daily (weekends too)
- Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep
- Track energy levels to find your optimal duration

### Pre-Sleep Relaxation (30 mins before bed)
- No screens or stimulating activities
- Try: reading, gentle stretching, meditation
- Keep bedroom cool, dark, and quiet
- Consider blackout curtains or white noise if needed

### Sleep Environment Checklist
- ✓ Cool temperature (65-68°F)
- ✓ Dark room
- ✓ Quiet space
- ✓ Comfortable mattress and pillows

## Pre/Post Workout Mindfulness

### Pre-Workout Visualization (2-3 mins)
- Sit quietly before starting
- Visualize yourself completing the workout successfully
- See yourself feeling strong and flexible
- Set a positive intention for the session

### Post-Workout Reflection (5 mins)
- How did your body feel during exercise?
- What felt good? What was challenging?
- Write down 1-2 observations about your progress
- Express gratitude to your body for showing up

---

## Hip Injury Management Mindfulness

### Body Scan Meditation (5 mins)
- Lie down comfortably
- Start at your toes, slowly move attention up your body
- When you reach your hip area, notice sensations without judgment
- Send kind, healing thoughts to that area
- Practice 2-3 times per week

### Gentle Movement Awareness
- During flexibility exercises, pay attention to your body's signals
- Distinguish between "good stretch" and "warning pain"
- Practice the phrase: "I listen to my body with kindness"
- Adjust movements consciously, not reactively

## Weekly Mindfulness Goals

### Week 1: Self-Acceptance
- **Daily Practice:** Each morning, look in the mirror and say one kind thing about your body
- **Goal:** Acknowledge challenges with your hip injury without harsh self-judgment
- **Mantra:** "I accept my body as it is today while working toward my goals"

### Week 2: Gratitude
- **Daily Practice:** End each day by writing down 3 things you're grateful for about your body
- **Goal:** Focus on what your body CAN do, not limitations
- **Mantra:** "My body is capable and deserving of care"

## Breathing Techniques for Flexibility

### Diaphragmatic Breathing
- Place one hand on chest, one on abdomen
- Inhale through nose, expanding abdomen (not chest)
- Exhale slowly through mouth
- **Use During:** All stretching exercises
- **Count:** 5 breaths minimum per stretch

### Extended Exhalation (4-2-6 Technique)
- Inhale for 4 counts
- Hold for 2 counts
- Exhale for 6 counts
- **Benefits:** Calms nervous system, enhances relaxation
- **Use During:** Deep stretches or when feeling tension

## Emergency Stress Relief

When feeling overwhelmed:
1. **STOP** what you're doing
2. **BREATHE** using 4-2-6 technique (3 rounds)
3. **GROUND** yourself by naming 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear
4. **AFFIRM** "This feeling will pass, I am safe"
