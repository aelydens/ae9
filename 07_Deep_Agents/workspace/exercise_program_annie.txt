### Comprehensive 2-Week Exercise Program for Annie
This 2-week exercise program is designed for Annie to improve flexibility and core strength while accommodating her hip injury. Key principles include:
- Focus on low-impact exercises that minimize stress on the hip.
- Gradual progression of exercises to build strength and endurance safely.
- Incorporation of warm-ups and cool-downs to enhance recovery and prevent injuries.
- Emphasis on listening to the body and modifying exercises as needed for comfort and safety.

### Week 1 Workout Schedule (3 Sessions)
**Session 1** (Monday)
- **Warm-up** (5 min):
  - Gentle seated torso twists: 2 min
  - Ankle circles (seated): 3 min (1 min each direction)

- **Main Workout** (20 min):
  - Cat-Cow Stretch: 3 sets of 8 reps
  - Seated Forward Bend: 2 sets of 30 sec hold
  - Side-lying leg raises (hip-neutral): 3 sets of 10 reps (use a pillow for support)
  - Glute bridges (1-leg, modified): 3 sets of 8 reps per side

- **Cool-down** (5 min):
  - Supine knee hugs: 2 min (1 min each side)
  - Deep breathing on back: 3 min

**Session 2** (Wednesday)
- **Warm-up** (5 min):
  - Gentle neck stretches: 2 min
  - Seated hamstring stretch: 3 min

- **Main Workout** (20 min):
  - Superman exercise: 3 sets of 8 reps
  - Seated side bends: 3 sets of 8 reps (each side)
  - Wall Sits (if comfortable): 3 sets of 20 sec hold
  - Butterfly stretch: 2 sets of 30 sec hold

- **Cool-down** (5 min):
  - Child's pose: 2 min
  - Seated deep breathing: 3 min

**Session 3** (Friday)
- **Warm-up** (5 min):
  - Arm circles: 2 min (1 min each direction)
  - Gentle hip circles while seated: 3 min

- **Main Workout** (20 min):
  - Bird-Dog: 3 sets of 6 reps per side
  - Lying leg lifts (hip-neutral): 3 sets of 10 reps
  - Seated twist (gentle): 3 sets of 8 reps per side
  - Wall push-ups: 3 sets of 8 reps

- **Cool-down** (5 min):
  - Reclined butterfly: 2 min
  - Lying down with deep breathing: 3 min

### Week 2 Workout Schedule (3 Sessions)
**Session 1** (Monday)
- **Warm-up** (5 min):
  - Gentle seated torso twists: 2 min
  - Ankle circles (seated): 3 min (1 min each direction)

- **Main Workout** (20 min):

  - Seated Forward Bend: 2 sets of 45 sec hold

  - Supine knee hugs: 2 min

  - Seated hamstring stretch: 3 min

- **Main Workout** (20 min):
  - Superman exercise: 3 sets of 10 reps
  - Seated side bends (increase range): 3 sets of 10 reps (each side)
  - Wall Sits: 3 sets of 30 sec hold
  - Butterfly stretch: 2 sets of 45 sec hold

- **Cool-down** (5 min):
  - Child's pose: 2 min
  - Seated deep breathing: 3 min

- **Warm-up** (5 min):
  - Arm circles: 2 min (1 min each direction)

- **Main Workout** (20 min):
  - Bird-Dog: 3 sets of 8 reps per side
  - Lying leg lifts: 3 sets of 12 reps
  - Seated twist: 3 sets of 10 reps per side
  - Wall push-ups: 3 sets of 10 reps

- **Cool-down** (5 min):
  - Reclined butterfly: 2 min
  - Lying down with deep breathing: 3 min

### Exercise Library
1. **Cat-Cow Stretch**: On hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding the spine. **Modification**: Perform seated if needed.
2. **Seated Forward Bend**: While seated, reach towards the toes to stretch hamstrings. **Modification**: Bend knees slightly.
3. **Side-lying Leg Raises**: Lying on your side, lift the top leg. **Modification**: Keep the leg straight to avoid hip strain.
4. **Glute Bridges**: Lying on your back, lift hips off the ground. **Modification**: Perform with 1 leg if full is uncomfortable.
5. **Superman Exercise**: Lying face down, lift arms and legs simultaneously. **Modification**: Lift one leg at a time.
6. **Seated Side Bends**: While seated, lean to each side to stretch. **Modification**: Keep the stretch gentle.
7. **Wall Sits**: Back against the wall, slide down to a sitting position. **Modification**: Reduce depth as needed.
8. **Butterfly Stretch**: Seat with soles of feet together, gently press knees down. **Modification**: Sit on a cushion for comfort.
9. **Bird-Dog**: On hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg. **Modification**: Keep foot on the ground if balance is needed.
10. **Wall Push-ups**: Lean against wall and push away. **Modification**: Stand closer for less intensity.

### Progression Notes and Tips
- Initially focus on form and range of motion, increasing rep counts or hold durations as comfort allows.
- Consider tracking improvements, such as ease of holding stretches or managing more reps.
- Regular hydration and breathing exercises will aid recovery and performance.

### Equipment List

- Cushion or pillow (for support)
