# Annie's 2-Week Exercise Plan
*Hip-Injury Friendly Flexibility & Core Strength Program*

## Program Overview and Principles
This 2-week exercise program is designed to improve flexibility and core strength while accommodating your hip injury. Key principles:
- Low-impact exercises that minimize hip stress
- Gradual progression for safe strength building
- Comprehensive warm-ups and cool-downs
- Listen to your body - modify as needed

**Schedule:** 3 sessions per week, 30 minutes each
**Best Time:** Afternoons (as preferred)

---

## Week 1 Workout Schedule

### Session 1 (Monday)
**Warm-up (5 min):**
- Gentle seated torso twists: 2 min
- Ankle circles (seated): 3 min (1 min each direction)

**Main Workout (20 min):**
- Cat-Cow Stretch: 3 sets of 8 reps
- Seated Forward Bend: 2 sets of 30 sec hold
- Side-lying leg raises (hip-neutral): 3 sets of 10 reps (use pillow for support)
- Glute bridges (modified): 3 sets of 8 reps per side

**Cool-down (5 min):**
- Supine knee hugs: 2 min (1 min each side)
- Deep breathing on back: 3 min

### Session 2 (Wednesday)
**Warm-up (5 min):**
- Gentle neck stretches: 2 min
- Seated hamstring stretch: 3 min

**Main Workout (20 min):**
- Superman exercise: 3 sets of 8 reps
- Seated side bends: 3 sets of 8 reps (each side)
- Wall Sits (if comfortable): 3 sets of 20 sec hold
- Butterfly stretch: 2 sets of 30 sec hold

**Cool-down (5 min):**
- Child's pose: 2 min
- Seated deep breathing: 3 min

### Session 3 (Friday)
**Warm-up (5 min):**
- Arm circles: 2 min (1 min each direction)
- Gentle hip circles while seated: 3 min

**Main Workout (20 min):**
- Bird-Dog: 3 sets of 6 reps per side
- Lying leg lifts (hip-neutral): 3 sets of 10 reps
- Seated twist (gentle): 3 sets of 8 reps per side
- Wall push-ups: 3 sets of 8 reps

**Cool-down (5 min):**
- Reclined butterfly: 2 min
- Lying down with deep breathing: 3 min

## Exercise Library with Hip Modifications

### 1. Cat-Cow Stretch
- **Standard:** On hands and knees, alternate arching and rounding spine
- **Hip Modification:** Perform seated if kneeling is uncomfortable

### 2. Seated Forward Bend
- **Standard:** Seated, reach toward toes
- **Hip Modification:** Bend knees slightly to reduce hip flexion

### 3. Side-lying Leg Raises
- **Standard:** Lying on side, lift top leg
- **Hip Modification:** Keep leg straight, use pillow for support, smaller range of motion

### 4. Glute Bridges
- **Standard:** Lying on back, lift hips off ground
- **Hip Modification:** Perform single-leg if full bridge causes discomfort

### 5. Superman Exercise
- **Standard:** Face down, lift arms and legs simultaneously
- **Hip Modification:** Lift one leg at a time or just upper body

### 6. Seated Side Bends
- **Standard:** Seated, lean to each side
- **Hip Modification:** Keep stretch gentle, don't force range

### 7. Wall Sits
- **Standard:** Back to wall, slide to sitting position
- **Hip Modification:** Reduce depth, stop before discomfort

### 8. Butterfly Stretch
- **Standard:** Seated, soles together, press knees down
- **Hip Modification:** Sit on cushion for comfort, don't force knees down

### 9. Bird-Dog
- **Standard:** Hands and knees, extend opposite arm/leg
- **Hip Modification:** Keep foot on ground if balance difficult

### 10. Wall Push-ups
- **Standard:** Lean against wall, push away
- **Hip Modification:** Stand closer to wall for less intensity

## Progression Notes & Tips
- Focus on form over quantity initially
- Track improvements in flexibility and strength
- Stay hydrated throughout workouts
- If hip pain occurs, stop and modify
- Celebrate small improvements!

## Equipment Needed
- Yoga mat (for comfort)
- Cushion or pillow (for support)
- Wall space (for wall exercises)

---

*Remember: Listen to your body and modify exercises as needed. Progress is measured not just in reps, but in how you feel and move!*
