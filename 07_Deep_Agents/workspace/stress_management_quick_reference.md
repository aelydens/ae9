# Stress Management Quick Reference Guide

## 5 Evidence-Based Strategies - Quick Overview

### 1. Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)
- **Practice**: 10-45 min daily meditation, body scanning, mindful movement
- **Benefits**: 25-50% stress reduction, improved sleep, better emotional regulation
- **Start**: Use meditation apps, begin with 10 min daily
- **Timeline**: Benefits appear in 4-8 weeks

### 2. Regular Aerobic Exercise
- **Practice**: 30-60 min moderate exercise, 3-5 times/week
- **Benefits**: Reduces stress hormones 20-30%, increases mood chemicals
- **Start**: 15-min walks, gradually increase duration
- **Timeline**: Benefits within 2-4 weeks

### 3. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Techniques
- **Practice**: Challenge negative thoughts, problem-solving strategies
- **Benefits**: 40-60% reduction in negative thinking patterns
- **Start**: Keep thought diary, challenge one negative thought daily
- **Timeline**: Results in 6-12 weeks

### 4. Deep Breathing Exercises
- **Practice**: 4-7-8 technique, diaphragmatic breathing, box breathing
- **Benefits**: Immediate stress relief, lower blood pressure
- **Start**: 5 minutes twice daily, use during stressful moments
- **Timeline**: Immediate benefits, improves with practice

### 5. Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)
- **Practice**: Systematically tense and release muscle groups
- **Benefits**: Reduces physical tension, improves sleep
- **Start**: 15-20 min sessions, practice before bedtime
- **Timeline**: Benefits after 1-2 weeks

## Daily Implementation Schedule

**Morning (10-15 min):**
- 5 min breathing/mindfulness
- 10 min exercise/stretching

**Workday:**
- 2-3 min breathing between meetings
- Brief mindful walking

**Evening (15-20 min):**
- PMR or gentle yoga
- Positive day reflection

## Emergency Stress Relief (2-5 minutes)
1. **4-7-8 Breathing**: 4 cycles for immediate calm
2. **Quick Muscle Release**: Tense and release shoulders/neck
3. **Mindful Grounding**: Name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you touch

## When to Seek Professional Help
- Stress impacts daily functioning
- Persistent anxiety/depression
- Physical symptoms don't improve
- Thoughts of self-harm

*Start with 1-2 techniques, practice consistently, be patient with results*
